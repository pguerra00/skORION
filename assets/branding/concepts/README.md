# scikitORION logo concepts

Four Art Deco taskbar-icon candidates generated on 2026-10-21 using the built-in image generation tool. These are candidates for selection; the application has not been configured to use any of them.

Palette reference: `gui_theme.py` — obsidian `#0A0A0A`, antique gold `#D4AF37`, warm ivory `#F2F0E4`, midnight blue `#1E3D59`. Generated colors may vary slightly; final production artwork can be normalized after selection.

Open `comparison.html` to compare large previews and icons at 16, 24, 32, and 48 CSS pixels on light and dark taskbars. The original generated PNGs are preserved without modification.

## 1. Orion Monogram

Octagonal gold O with three ivory diamonds suggesting Orion’s belt. Recommended starting point for its simple silhouette.

Image: `01-orion-monogram.png`

### Final generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: scikitORION desktop application taskbar icon concept, one single square icon, 1024x1024.
Brand context: scientific desktop software for microscopy nuclear-foci analysis. Its actual GUI has a restrained 1920s Art Deco / Gatsby aesthetic: architectural geometry, precise symmetry, chamfered corners, diamond motifs.
Color palette from the actual GUI: obsidian #0A0A0A, antique gold #D4AF37, warm ivory #F2F0E4, midnight blue #1E3D59; gold is the dominant mark color. Use flat exact colors, crisp clean vector-like raster edges.
Composition: ONE centered standalone icon, large and prominent, on an obsidian square tile with slightly chamfered corners. Tile occupies 88% of square canvas, transparent outside tile. Main symbol occupies about 70% of tile. Balanced negative space.
Production constraints: Strong distinctive silhouette designed to survive reduction to 24-48 pixels. Bold strokes and solid shapes, no tiny detail, no hairlines, at most 5 main visual components. Flat front view. No words, labels, numbers, title, wordmark, mockup, border beyond described form, gradients, metallic texture, 3D bevel, shadows, glow, watermark, extra decorative stars, or surrounding scene. This is an actual icon asset, not a presentation board.
Primary request: An elegant geometric capital O emblem with a broad antique-gold band and eight-sided chamfered Art Deco outer and inner contours. Across its open dark center, place exactly three bold small warm-ivory diamond shapes arranged in a rising diagonal, evoking Orion's belt. The O remains clearly readable. The three diamonds are large enough to read at small sizes and are separate from the O band. Simple, iconic, architectural.
```
